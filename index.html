02 The Approach

The quiet
product machine
model.

Most builders chase distribution through personal brand performance — social posting, content calendars, follower counts. I don't. Every product in this catalog generates its own traffic through search. Code handles the distribution. I handle the architecture.

The result is a portfolio of automated systems that compound over time, require minimal ongoing effort, and run on zero-cost infrastructure. No paid hosting. No social dependency.

01

Search over social

Traffic comes from Google, not from posting. Programmatic SEO generates hundreds of indexed pages automatically from structured data.

02

Zero fixed costs

Every system runs on GitHub Pages, GitHub Actions, and free-tier APIs. Monthly infrastructure cost across all products: $0.

03

Automation over maintenance

Pipelines rebuild content automatically. Products update themselves. Human intervention is for architecture, not operations.

04

Ship, then compound

Each product adds to a growing surface area of indexable content and backlinks. The portfolio compounds; each product benefits from the rest.
