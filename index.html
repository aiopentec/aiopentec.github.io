The Approach

The quiet product machine model.

Most builders chase distribution through personal brand performance — social posting, content calendars, follower counts. Every product here generates its own traffic through search. Code handles the distribution. I handle the architecture.

The result is a portfolio of automated systems that compound over time, require minimal ongoing effort, and run on zero-cost infrastructure.

🔍
Search over social
Traffic comes from Google, not posting. Programmatic SEO generates hundreds of indexed pages automatically from structured data.
💰
Zero fixed costs
Every system runs on GitHub Pages, GitHub Actions, and free-tier APIs. Monthly infrastructure cost across all products: $0.
⚙️
Automation over maintenance
Pipelines rebuild content automatically. Products update themselves. Human effort goes to architecture, not operations.
📈
Ship, then compound
Each product adds to a growing surface area of indexable content and backlinks. The portfolio compounds — each product benefits from the rest.
