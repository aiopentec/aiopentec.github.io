The structure, in 10 seconds

Every strong CV bullet follows Action + Task + Result — the format used throughout the UK/US Jobs Guide:

EXAMPLE — FROM THE GUIDE
Owned a book of 40+ enterprise accounts, driving $1.2M in renewals.
✓ Do
  • Start with a strong action verb — one tap below (or type your own)
  • Include a real number — size, %, currency, time, volume
  • Keep it under 25 words (the ATS Breakthrough Pack rule)
  • Keep every claim truthful — no invented metrics
✕ Swap out
  • responsible for · duties included · worked on
  • helped with · tasked with · involved in
  • team player · hardworking · various
  • First-person words (I, my, we) — CV convention drops them
