UK Skilled Worker Salary 2026: What Nigerian Applicants Need to Know

UK VISA · 2026
PUBLISHED 2026 · 6 MIN READ · FROM THE UK/US JOBS GUIDE, SOURCE NOTES
£41,700 standard£33,400 concession floor£25,000 health & careCare workers: overseas route closed

If you're applying for UK roles from Nigeria in 2026, four numbers decide whether a job offer can actually become a visa. This post summarizes them plainly — and shows you how to verify each one on the official source, because rules change and no blog post (including this one) should ever be your final authority.

1 — The standard threshold: £41,700 (or the going rate)

Under current GOV.UK guidance, the standard Skilled Worker salary requirement is the higher of £41,700 per year or the occupation's "going rate" for that specific job. Some applicants and occupations qualify for different rules — which is why the occupation code on your Certificate of Sponsorship matters as much as the salary itself.

Practical takeaway: when you see a job posting, check the salary against both figures. A role paying £43,000 clears the standard threshold only if it also meets the going rate for its occupation code.

2 — The lower route: £33,400 floor (for some applicants)

Certain applicants — including some under 26, recent graduates, and relevant STEM PhD holders — may qualify for a lower salary route, subject to exact conditions. GOV.UK currently describes a £33,400 floor for that route.

Practical takeaway: if you're a recent graduate or hold a relevant STEM PhD, don't self-reject from roles in the £33K–£41K band. Check the conditions on GOV.UK first.

3 — Health & Care roles: £25,000 baseline

Eligible healthcare roles follow different salary rules — some use a £25,000 minimum or the applicable national-pay-scale going rate; other eligible occupations use different lower-rate rules. Always check the occupation code and current going rate for the specific role.

4 — The 2026 care-worker change

This is the one that surprises people: new overseas applications for care worker and senior care worker jobs are no longer permitted under the current UK route. Some people already in the UK can switch under specified transitional rules — but if you're applying from outside the UK, plan around this reality rather than an agent's promise.

What this means for your targeting

These numbers should shape your 60-employer target list before you write a single application:

  • Verify the sponsor — only employers on the official register of licensed sponsors can sponsor you. Check every target.
  • Check the occupation code — the going rate and route depend on it, not just the job title.
  • Match salary to route — your CV's value story should align with the salary band the route requires.
  • Keep documents consistent — job title, occupation code, salary and employer details must match across everything.
⚠ Scam warning

Never pay for a guaranteed visa. Red flags: fake job letters, requests for untraceable payment, guaranteed approval claims, instructions to falsify documents. Report fraud via the official channels listed below.

✓ Your next step

Write down your target country, target role, target salary and target industry — then verify your prospective sponsor on the official register before investing a single hour in the application.
