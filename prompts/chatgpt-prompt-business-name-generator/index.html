Business Name Generator

Brand Naming

Generates 30 name ideas across six naming styles — descriptive, invented, compound, metaphor, founder, and acronym — each with a pros/cons note and a tagline suggestion, instead of one flat list.

Best for: Anyone naming a new business, product, or side project who wants real range instead of thirty variations on the same idea.

PROMPT Nº 23 OF 37

Generate business name ideas for [BUSINESS DESCRIPTION].

BUSINESS INFO:
  • Industry: [your industry]
  • Products/Services: [what you offer]
  • Target audience: [who you serve]
  • Brand personality: [adjectives: modern, trustworthy, playful, etc.]
  • Key values: [what you stand for]
GENERATE 30 NAME IDEAS across these categories:
  1. Descriptive names (5) - clearly state what you do
  2. Invented names (5) - made-up words, brandable
  3. Compound names (5) - two words combined
  4. Metaphor names (5) - symbolic, evocative
  5. Founder names (5) - personal touch
  6. Acronym names (5) - initials that mean something
For EACH name provide:
  • The name
  • .com domain availability (assume - I'll verify)
  • Pros (why it works)
  • Cons (potential issues)
  • Tagline suggestion

Prioritize: memorable, easy to spell, not too similar to existing brands.
