Travel Itinerary

Trip Planning

Plans a full day-by-day trip itinerary — morning activity, lunch, afternoon plan, dinner, and transport between each — plus pre-trip prep, budget notes, and local etiquette, based on your actual dates and pace.

Best for: Anyone planning a trip who wants a real day-by-day plan instead of a list of attractions to figure out later.

PROMPT Nº 30 OF 37

Plan a complete travel itinerary for [DESTINATION].

TRIP DETAILS:
  • Destination: [city/country]
  • Duration: [number of days]
  • Travel dates: [dates if known]
  • Travelers: [solo/couple/family/group]
  • Budget: [budget/mid-range/luxury]
  • Interests: [food/history/nature/adventure/culture/shopping]
  • Pace: [relaxed/moderate/packed]
FOR EACH DAY CREATE:
  • Morning activity (with address, hours, cost)
  • Lunch spot (with reservation tips)
  • Afternoon activity
  • Dinner recommendation
  • Evening option
  • Getting around (transport between spots)
  • Time estimates
  • Backup options (if weather bad)
ALSO INCLUDE:
  1. Pre-trip prep (visa, vaccinations, packing list)
  2. Where to stay (area recommendations)
  3. Money tips (budget breakdown)
  4. Safety considerations
  5. Local customs/etiquette
  6. Essential phrases (if language barrier)
  7. Hidden gems (off-beaten-path spots)
  8. Food must-tries

Make it balanced - not too rushed.
