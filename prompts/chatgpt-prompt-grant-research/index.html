Grant Research

Opportunity Finder

Surfaces federal, state, private, and corporate grant opportunities for a specific project, then scores each by fit, deadline, and competition level so you apply to the best matches first.

Best for: Nonprofits, researchers, and small businesses that need funding and don't have time to comb grant databases by hand.

PROMPT Nº 33 OF 37

Find grant opportunities for [ORGANIZATION/PROJECT].

ORGANIZATION:
  • Type: [nonprofit/research/small business/etc.]
  • Mission: [what you do]
  • Location: [city, state]
  • Annual budget: [size]
PROJECT SEEKING FUNDING:
  • Description: [what needs funding]
  • Budget needed: [amount]
  • Timeline: [when needed]
  • Impact: [who/what benefits]
RESEARCH:
  1. Federal Grants (list 5-10 with eligibility)
  2. State/Local Grants (list 5-10)
  3. Private Foundations (list 10-15)
  4. Corporate Giving Programs (list 5-10)
FOR EACH GRANT PROVIDE:
  • Grant name and funder
  • Funding amount (range)
  • Eligibility criteria
  • Deadline
  • Competition level (low/med/high)
  • Match percentage (fit to our project: %)
  • Application requirements
  • Success tips

Prioritize by likelihood of success + funding amount.
