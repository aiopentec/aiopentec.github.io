Business Proposal

Client Project

Builds a ten-part client proposal — cover letter, executive summary, scope of work, timeline, pricing, credentials, and next steps — framed around the client's outcome rather than a features list.

Best for: Consultants, agencies, and freelancers who send custom proposals and want a strong starting draft per client.

PROMPT Nº 20 OF 37

Create a business proposal for [CLIENT] for [PROJECT].

PROJECT DETAILS:
  • Client name: [company/person]
  • Project: [what they need]
  • Their goals: [what they want to achieve]
  • Timeline: [deadline or duration]
  • Your proposed solution: [your approach]
  • Investment: [your pricing]
PROPOSAL STRUCTURE:
  1. Cover letter (warm, personal, 150 words)
  2. Executive Summary (the win for them, 100 words)
  3. Understanding of Need (show you get their problem, 150 words)
  4. Proposed Solution (your approach, 200 words)
  5. Scope of Work (detailed deliverables, bullet points)
  6. Timeline (phases with dates)
  7. Investment (pricing breakdown)
  8. Why Us (credentials, past results, 100 words)
  9. Next Steps (clear CTA)
  10. Terms & Conditions (payment, revisions, etc.)

Make it benefits-focused, not features-focused.
