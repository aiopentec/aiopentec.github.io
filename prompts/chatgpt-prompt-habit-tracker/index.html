Habit Formation

30-Day Challenge

Designs a 30-day habit plan that starts deliberately small and ramps weekly, with a daily tracker, trigger design, a reward system, and an if-then plan for when you inevitably miss a day.

Best for: Anyone who has tried and dropped a habit before and wants a plan built around actually sticking with it.

PROMPT Nº 29 OF 37

Design a 30-day habit-building challenge for [HABIT].

  • HABIT: [what they want to build]
  • WHY: [their motivation]
  • CURRENT SITUATION: [starting point]
  • OBSTACLES: [what might get in the way]
CREATE:
  1. Habit Stacking (link to existing habit)
  2. 30-Day Progressive Plan:
    • Week 1: [mini version of habit]
    • Week 2: [slight increase]
    • Week 3: [full habit]
    • Week 4: [solidify]
  3. Daily Checklist/Tracker
  4. Trigger Design (what reminds them)
  5. Reward System (celebrate wins)
  6. Obstacle Solutions (if-then plans)
  7. Accountability Method
  8. Relapse Recovery Plan
For EACH DAY provide:
  • The specific action
  • Time commitment
  • Difficulty (easy/medium/hard)
  • Motivational reminder

Make it start ridiculously easy to ensure early wins.
