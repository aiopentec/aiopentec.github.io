8 rules for 10X output.

01 · BE SPECIFIC

"1500-word SEO post about email marketing for small businesses" beats "write a blog post."

Audience, goals, constraints. The AI can't read your mind — yet.

03 · USE EXAMPLES

Show what you want. And what you don't. Examples are shortcuts.

Sequence multiple prompts for complex, multi-part projects.

Request 3–5 options, then pick. Choice beats settling.
