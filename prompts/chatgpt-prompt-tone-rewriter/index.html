Tone Adjustment

Rewrite Content

Rewrites the same piece of text in six different tones — professional, casual, witty, authoritative, empathetic, and urgent — at roughly the same length, so you can pick what actually fits the audience.

Best for: Anyone reusing content across different audiences who needs the tone adjusted without rewriting from scratch each time.

PROMPT Nº 37 OF 37

Rewrite this content in different tones.

ORIGINAL TEXT:
  • [Paste your content]
REWRITE IN THESE TONES:
  1. Professional/Corporate
  2. Casual/Conversational
  3. Witty/Humorous
  4. Authoritative/Expert
  5. Empathetic/Warm
  6. Urgent/Action-oriented
For each version:
  • Maintain core message
  • Adjust vocabulary
  • Change sentence structure
  • Modify examples if needed
  • Keep same length (approximately)
