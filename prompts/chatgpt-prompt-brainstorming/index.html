Brainstorming

Idea Generator

Generates 20 ideas for one problem across four distinct thinking methods — conventional, unconventional, combination, and reversed-assumption — then ranks all of them by impact times feasibility.

Best for: Anyone stuck on a problem who wants genuinely varied ideas instead of twenty versions of the same obvious answer.

PROMPT Nº 34 OF 37

I need creative ideas for [TOPIC/PROBLEM].

CONTEXT:
  • What I'm working on: [describe]
  • Goal: [what success looks like]
  • Constraints: [limitations - budget, time, resources]
  • Target audience: [who it's for]

Generate 20 ideas using these thinking methods:

  • 5 conventional ideas (tried and tested)
  • 5 unconventional ideas (outside the box)
  • 5 combination ideas (merge existing concepts)
  • 5 opposite ideas (reverse assumptions)
For each idea provide:
  • The core concept (one sentence)
  • Why it could work
  • Potential obstacles
  • Difficulty to implement (1-10)
  • Potential impact (1-10)

Then rank all 20 by: Impact × Feasibility.
