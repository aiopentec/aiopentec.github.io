Sales Script

Discovery Call

Builds a full discovery call script timed by section — rapport, agenda, 10-15 discovery questions, solution presentation, objection handling, and close — with the transition phrases between sections included.

Best for: Founders and salespeople who run their own discovery calls and want structure without sounding scripted.

PROMPT Nº 25 OF 37

Create a discovery call script for selling [YOUR SERVICE/PRODUCT].

OFFER INFO:
  • Service: [what you sell]
  • Price range: [your pricing]
  • Ideal client: [who needs this]
  • Common objections: [list 3-5]
SCRIPT STRUCTURE:
  1. Opening (build rapport, 2 min)
  2. Set agenda (what we'll cover, 1 min)
  3. Discovery questions (10-15 questions, 15 min)
  4. Present solution (based on their answers, 10 min)
  5. Address objections (handle pushback, 5 min)
  6. Close (ask for the sale, 3 min)
  7. Next steps (if yes or no, 2 min)
For DISCOVERY QUESTIONS include:
  • Open-ended questions about their situation
  • Pain point questions
  • Budget qualification
  • Decision-making process
  • Timeline questions
  • Success criteria
Include:
  • Transition phrases between sections
  • Objection handling scripts
  • Closing questions

Make it conversational, not rigid.
