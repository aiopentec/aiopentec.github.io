Content Repurposing

Multi-Platform

Takes one piece of content — a blog post, video script, or podcast episode — and adapts it into eight formats: a Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, IG carousel, reel script, email section, and more, each tone-matched to its platform.

Best for: Solo creators and small content teams who publish once but want that one piece working across every channel.

PROMPT Nº 36 OF 37

Repurpose this [CONTENT TYPE] into multiple formats.

ORIGINAL CONTENT:
  • [Paste your content: blog post, video script, podcast episode, etc.]
  • ORIGINAL FORMAT: [what it is now]
  • TARGET AUDIENCE: [who it's for]
REPURPOSE INTO:
  1. Twitter thread (10 tweets)
  2. LinkedIn post (1500 words)
  3. Instagram carousel (8 slides with text)
  4. TikTok/Reel script (60 seconds)
  5. Email newsletter section (300 words)
  6. Infographic outline (key data points)
  7. Podcast talking points (5 main points)
  8. Quote graphics (5 pull quotes)
For each format:
  • Adapt tone for platform
  • Include engagement hooks
  • Optimize for platform algorithm
  • Add relevant hashtags/keywords

Make each piece standalone while maintaining core message.
