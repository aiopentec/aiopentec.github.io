Scam Detection

Offer Analysis

Walks an investment or business opportunity through a red-flag checklist — business model, claims verification, legitimacy signals — and ends with a plain risk rating and recommendation.

Best for: Anyone evaluating an offer, investment, or opportunity that feels uncertain and wants a structured second opinion before committing.

PROMPT Nº 32 OF 37

Analyze this [INVESTMENT/OFFER/OPPORTUNITY] for legitimacy and red flags.

OFFER DETAILS:
  • [Paste offer information, sales page, pitch, etc.]
COMPANY/PERSON BEHIND IT:
  • [Name, background if known]
ANALYZE:
  1. Business Model Analysis
    • How do they make money?
    • Is it sustainable?
    • Who pays whom?
  2. Claims Verification
    • What specific claims are made?
    • Are they realistic?
    • Any proof provided?
  3. Red Flag Checklist (check for):
    • Guaranteed returns
    • Pressure tactics
    • Unclear fee structure
    • Too good to be true promises
    • Unverifiable testimonials
    • Complex compensation plans
    • Limited-time pressure
    • Request for upfront payment
  4. Legitimacy Check
    • Company registration status
    • Better Business Bureau rating
    • Online reviews (patterns in complaints)
    • Social media presence authenticity
    • Leadership background
  5. Risk Assessment (1-10 scale)
  6. Recommendation (avoid/proceed with caution/seems legit)

Be objective and thorough.
