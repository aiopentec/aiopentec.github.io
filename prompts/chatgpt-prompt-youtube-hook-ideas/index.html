YouTube Script

Hook Variations

Generates ten different opening-15-second hooks for the same video idea, across question hooks, shocking statements, curiosity gaps, and more, and explains the psychological trigger behind each one.

Best for: Creators who know their video topic but always get stuck on the first line that keeps people from scrolling past.

PROMPT Nº 06 OF 37

Create 10 different opening hooks (first 15 seconds) for a YouTube video about [TOPIC].

  • VIDEO PREMISE: [brief description]
For each hook provide:
  1. The exact words to say
  2. Suggested visual/B-roll
  3. Why this hook works (psychological trigger used)
HOOK TYPES TO INCLUDE:
  • Question hook
  • Shocking statement
  • Personal story teaser
  • "Avoid this mistake" hook
  • Results preview
  • Curiosity gap
  • Pattern interrupt
  • Controversy/debate
  • Before/after
  • Direct value proposition

Make them punchy and skip-proof.
